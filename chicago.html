Chicago notes / home base

Chicago is part of how I introduce myself.

I was born and raised here, and I still see the city as an open invitation: find a new corner, meet someone for food, and make a regular afternoon feel like a small adventure.

Things I like doing here

My favorite plans are simple.

I don’t need a packed itinerary. I like having a destination, leaving room for a detour, and bringing people I can talk to about almost anything.

01

Find a new food spot

Food is one of my favorite ways to explore Chicago. A new neighborhood usually means a new place I want to try.

Bonus points if the order is impossible to eat neatly.
02

Make a day of it

I like plans that start with one idea and get better when we wander—walking, talking, and finding something none of us expected.

The best schedule has some empty space.
03

Share the city

Chicago feels most like home when I’m showing someone a place I enjoy or letting a friend choose the next stop.

My city recommendation list is always in progress.
Chicago detail: I’m proud that “where are you from?” is an easy question for me to answer.
