Gym notes / chest day

Chest day is the best day. I will defend that position.

The gym gives me a routine that is simple to return to. I’m still refining the exact split, but I know the feeling I’m chasing: show up, do the work, and leave a little stronger.

The split I’m building around

A framework, not a rulebook.

This is the structure I like training around right now. I’m still learning what works best for me, so the split can change as I learn more.

01

Push / chest first

Chest, shoulders, and triceps. This is the day I look forward to most, especially when incline bench is on the plan.

Chest day is the best day.
02

Pull / back and biceps

Pulling work balances the week and gives me a different kind of challenge from the sessions I naturally want to repeat.

Consistency over chasing a perfect session.
03

Legs / no negotiations

Leg day is the reminder that a favorite workout cannot be the only workout. I show up, do what I can, and keep the split honest.

The hardest part is usually starting.
04

Running / complicated feelings

I have a love-hate relationship with running. I rarely feel excited before it, but I’m usually glad I finished.

Still working on the “love” part.
My gym principle: the routine does not need to be perfect to be worth returning to.
